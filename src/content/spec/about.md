# About UrbanVPN

**UrbanVPN** is an independent, next-generation digital privacy platform dedicated to providing open, unfiltered, and deeply secure internet access for users worldwide.

## Our Mission

In an era of ubiquitous tracking, algorithmic surveillance, and fragmented network accessibility, digital privacy is not a luxury—it is a fundamental human right. UrbanVPN is engineered to deliver zero-compromise encryption, exceptional throughput, and transparent privacy architecture.

## Core Architectural Principles

- **Zero-Logs Architecture**: No activity logs, connection timestamps, IP assignments, or DNS request queries are ever stored on disk or transferred to third parties.
- **RAM-Only Node Infrastructure**: All routing edge servers operate exclusively in volatile RAM. No persistent disk writes occur, guaranteeing that any physical power interruption instantly purges all volatile memory.
- **Modern Cryptographic Protocols**: Utilizing WireGuard and cutting-edge post-quantum symmetric encryption (ChaCha20-Poly1305, Curve25519) to ensure minimal handshake latency and maximum resistance against forward cryptanalysis.
- **DNS Leak Prevention & IPv6 Isolation**: Autonomous private DNS resolvers running directly on every edge node prevent ISP intercept and telemetry leakage.

## Global Edge Network

Our edge network spans tier-1 transit providers across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and emerging regions, routing traffic through low-latency backbone cross-connects designed for high-concurrency streaming, secure communication, and distributed remote workflows.

## Commitment to Open Standards

We actively support and advocate for open-source privacy tooling, net neutrality, and user sovereignty across the global web.
